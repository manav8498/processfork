# Code of Conduct

ProcessFork follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

The short version:

- Be kind. Assume good faith.
- Disagreement is welcome; harassment is not.
- The maintainers reserve the right to remove contributions and ban
  participants who violate this code.

Report incidents to **conduct@processfork.dev**. Reports are confidential.
